FAQ

Why not use a template?

Because a custom website is designed around what your business needs. We use proven tools and components, but the design, structure and copy are made for you.

Can I edit the website myself?

It depends on the project. If you want to change content yourself, we include it in the quote; if not, we take care of it with maintenance.

What about maintenance?

It is quoted according to the scope of the website and what you want us to do each month. SEO, Google Business Profile and ads are separate.

Do we need to meet?

No. We work remotely on WhatsApp, email or video call, wherever you are.

Do you guarantee results?

No. We build the website to work and to be understood by search engines, but no website can guarantee rankings, visits or sales.
