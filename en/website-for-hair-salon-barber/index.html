What a hair salon website needs

People looking for a hairdresser decide quickly: price, opening hours, photos and how to book.

Clear services and prices

Cuts, colour, highlights, beard, treatments… with the price or a "from" price, so customers know what to expect without having to ask.

Easy booking

A WhatsApp button to book. If you already use a booking app, we link the button to it so you don't have to change how you work.

Photos of your work and your salon

Real cuts and styles, and what the salon looks like inside. Together with opening hours and a map, that's what reassures a new customer most.

Before we build your salon's website

Three things worth being clear about before we start. They are what we get asked most, and what makes the biggest difference to the messages you receive afterwards.

What people search for

People looking for a hairdresser on Google usually type the service and the area: “hair salon near me”, “barber” plus the name of the neighbourhood, “balayage highlights”, “kids' haircut”. That is why your website names each service the way your customers say it, not only the way you call it, and makes it clear which neighbourhood or town you are in.

What we need from you

  • Your list of services with prices or “from” prices. A photo of the price list in your salon is fine.
  • Your opening hours, including days you close at lunchtime or Saturdays you open.
  • Between 8 and 12 photos: the inside of the salon, the shopfront so people recognise it, and your own work, with permission from any clients whose faces are shown.
  • How people book today: WhatsApp, phone or the booking app you use.
  • Your Instagram and Google Business Profile, if you have them.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Stock photos: customers recognise them and trust the business less. Your own photos are better, even if taken on a phone in good light.
  • Out-of-date prices: if the salon charges something different, customers feel misled. When prices change, tell us and we update them.
  • A hidden booking button: it should be visible on a phone screen without scrolling.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a hair salon website cost?

Build: €490 + VAT (€592.90 including VAT), in two payments of €245 + VAT. Includes design, copywriting, a .com or .es domain, hosting and maintenance for the first year. From the second year, €35 + VAT a month (€42.35), with no minimum term.

Does it include a booking system?

The one-page website doesn't include its own booking system. Customers book via WhatsApp or, if you already use a booking app, we link the button to it. If you need something more complete, we quote it separately before starting. If you want customers to book on their own, see our website with online bookings.

Can I change my prices whenever I want?

Yes. During the first year, small changes are covered by maintenance; just ask us on WhatsApp and we'll make them.

I already have Instagram, why do I need a website?

Instagram shows your day-to-day work, but not everyone uses it, and opening hours, prices or how to book aren't always easy to find there. The website sums it all up on one page and links to your Instagram.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. Everything is done over WhatsApp and email: you tell us about your salon, send us photos and review the website on your phone. It doesn't matter where in Spain you are.

Do you guarantee customers or the top spot on Google?

No. We set the website up so search engines understand it (titles, description and business details), but no website can guarantee rankings, visits or sales.

A simple website for a hair salon or barber shop

Many people look for a new hairdresser when they move, when their usual one closes or when they want to try something different. At that moment they compare two or three places on their phone and choose the one that feels trustworthy and makes booking easy.

That's why a hair salon website has to get to the point: what services you offer, what they cost, when you're open, where you are and how to book. With that and some good photos, customers have everything they need to decide.
