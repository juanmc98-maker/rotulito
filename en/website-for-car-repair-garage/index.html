What a garage website needs

For most garages one page is enough: just what a customer needs to see you're the right garage and get in touch. If you offer very different services and want each one to show up on Google separately, a multi-page website is a better fit.

Your services, clearly

General mechanics, oil changes, brakes, tyres, diagnostics… whatever you do, explained without jargon so customers quickly know whether you can help.

Contact in one tap

Call and WhatsApp buttons always visible on mobile. Customers can send you a photo of the problem or book in without waiting for you to pick up the phone.

Opening hours and directions

Address with a map, opening hours and whether you work by appointment. The basics, so nobody turns up when you're closed.

What drivers ask before bringing the car in

These are the calls that catch you with oily hands. If the website answers them, people call you less to ask and more to book.

“Can you look at it today?”

Whether you work by appointment or take walk-ins, and what to do if the car won't start or needs towing.

“How much is an oil change?”

"From" prices for the usual jobs, if you want to publish them, and an invitation to send a photo or the number plate by WhatsApp for a quote.

“Do you work on my make?”

The makes or types of vehicle you work on (cars, vans, motorbikes, hybrids…) and what you don't do.

“Do you do pre-MOT checks?”

One line for each service people search for by name: pre-ITV inspection check, air conditioning, diagnostics, tyres.

“Do you have a courtesy car?”

If you offer one, or whether there is public transport nearby. People ask this before leaving the car for a whole day.

“What time can I pick it up?”

Clear opening hours, including summer hours and holiday closures, which we change when you let us know.

Which sections a garage website needs (and why)

All on one page, designed to be read on a phone, often from the street or from the car itself.

  1. Home section with what you do and whereGeneral repairs, auto electrics, tyres… and the neighbourhood or industrial estate. Anyone who finds you on the map can confirm at a glance that you are the garage they were looking for.
  2. Services with guide pricesServicing, brakes, tyres, pre-ITV check, air conditioning, diagnostics. With "from" prices if you like, so people don't call just to ask.
  3. Photos of the garageThe lift, the work area, reception. A tidy garage in the photos tells people their car will be looked after.
  4. Booking and quotes by WhatsAppSo customers can send a photo of the warning light or the dent, plus a call button for anything urgent.
  5. Opening hours and directionsWith a map and a button to get there. If you are on an industrial estate, a note on how to find the unit.
  6. About youHow long you have been going, who looks after customers and which makes you work on. No frills.

To prepare it we only need photos of the garage taken with your phone, your list of services (with prices if you want to show them), your opening hours and your WhatsApp number.

Frequently asked questions

Can I show my prices?

Yes, if you want. You can show fixed prices, "from" prices or no prices at all and let customers ask for a quote on WhatsApp. It's your choice, and changing a price later is covered by maintenance.

Is there any garage-specific information to include?

As well as the legal notice, privacy and cookies, you can show your garage registration number and remind customers that you give a written quote before any repair, which garage regulations in Spain recognise as a customer right. We add it with the details you give us; if in doubt, your trade association can confirm it.

Can customers book from the website?

Yes, with the WhatsApp and call buttons. The one-page website doesn't include a booking system with a calendar; if you need one, we look at it separately and before starting. If you want customers to book on their own, see our website with online bookings.

I also do bodywork and paint, or motorbikes. Does it all fit?

Yes. Each speciality goes in its own block on the same page. If you want each one to appear on Google separately with its own page, a multi-page website suits you better, and we will tell you before we start.

Can you post a notice when we close for holidays?

Yes. You tell us by WhatsApp, we add a notice to the home section and change the opening hours. It is a small change included in maintenance.
