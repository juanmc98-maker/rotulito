What a garage website needs

For most garages one page is enough: just what a customer needs to see you're the right garage and get in touch. If you offer very different services and want each one to show up on Google separately, a multi-page website is a better fit.

Your services, clearly

General mechanics, oil changes, brakes, tyres, diagnostics… whatever you do, explained without jargon so customers quickly know whether you can help.

Contact in one tap

Call and WhatsApp buttons always visible on mobile. Customers can send you a photo of the problem or book in without waiting for you to pick up the phone.

Opening hours and directions

Address with a map, opening hours and whether you work by appointment. The basics, so nobody turns up when you're closed.

How it works, in 3 steps

The whole process happens over WhatsApp and email, with no meetings. You tell us about your garage; we do the rest.

1

You tell us about your garage

Fill in the form or message us on WhatsApp. We confirm whether a one-page website is what you need and put the price, maintenance and what's included in writing. Before you pay anything, we show you a homepage proposal at no cost: if you like it, you pay 50 % and we start; if not, there is no order and no payment.

2

You send photos of the garage from your phone (up to 12), your logo if you have one, your services and contact details. We write the copy and send you the finished site to review. Up to two rounds of changes.

Once you approve it, you pay the remaining 50 % and we go live: we register your domain, publish the site, link it to your Google listing if you have one and submit it to Google for indexing. Then we explain how to ask us for changes.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a garage website cost?

Build: €490 + VAT (€592.90 including VAT), in two payments of €245 + VAT. Includes design, copy, a .com or .es domain, hosting and maintenance for the first year. From year two, €35 + VAT per month (€42.35), no lock-in.

Can I show my prices?

Yes, if you want. You can show fixed prices, "from" prices or no prices at all and let customers ask for a quote on WhatsApp. It's your choice, and changing a price later is covered by maintenance.

Can customers book from the website?

Yes, with the WhatsApp and call buttons. The one-page website doesn't include a booking system with a calendar; if you need one, we look at it separately and before starting. If you want customers to book on their own, see our website with online bookings.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. Everything is done over WhatsApp and email: you tell us about your garage, send photos and review the site on your phone. It doesn't matter where in Spain you are.

Is the domain mine?

Yes. It's registered in your name. If you decide to leave, you take it with you.

Do you guarantee customers or first place on Google?

No. We prepare the site so search engines understand it (titles, description and business details), but no website can guarantee rankings, visits or sales.

A simple website for a garage

When a warning light comes on or a service is due, people don't look for the prettiest website: they look for a garage they can trust, that's close by and that answers quickly. Your garage's website has to sort that out in a few seconds.

That's why we make it a single page: who you are, what services you offer, opening hours, where you are and the call and WhatsApp buttons. If you have a Google listing, we link them so people who find you on the map reach your website and vice versa.

We work remotely with businesses all over Spain: you send us photos of the garage on WhatsApp and we write the copy.
