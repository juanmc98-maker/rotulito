What makes the price go up or down

Two websites can cost very different amounts for these three reasons:

How many pages and features

A simple presentation page doesn't cost the same as an online shop, a booking system or a customer area.

Who builds it

Doing it yourself on a platform, hiring a freelancer or an agency: the price changes, and so does the time you have to put in yourself.

What's included

Copy, photos, domain, hosting and maintenance are sometimes included and sometimes charged separately. Always compare the total, not just the build price.

What changes from one website to another

Two websites with the same label can be very different jobs. Before comparing prices, check what each one includes:

  • DesignAn adapted template or a design made for your business.
  • PagesA single page, 3-5 pages or one page for each service, area or product.
  • CopyWhether you write it or it is written for you. Trustlocal lists copywriting as a separate service.
  • CatalogueProduct or project listings that can be filtered and updated.
  • Content managementWhether you will be able to edit the site yourself or the developer makes the changes.
  • IntegrationsBookings, payments, CRM, document generation or other systems.
  • LanguagesEach language multiplies copy, review and maintenance.
  • SupportWhat happens after launch: changes, backups, renewals and who you call.

Frequently asked questions

When is a one-page website enough?

When what you need is to present your business, your services and how to contact you: garages, trades, hair salons, small shops or professionals. If you sell online, publish a lot of content or have very different services to rank separately, a larger website suits you better; we look at it before starting and, if that's your case, we tell you.

What costs do I have every year?

At the very least, domain renewal and hosting. If you want someone to make changes for you, maintenance. In our case, everything is included in year one, and from year two it's €20 + VAT a month (domain, hosting and up to 30 minutes a month of small changes), no lock-in.

Is it worth it if I already have Instagram and Google Maps?

They complement each other. Instagram and your Google listing help a lot, but not everyone uses Instagram and on neither of them do you fully control how your business is presented. The website sums it up on a page that's yours and can link to both.

What if I build it myself on a platform?

That's a valid option if you have time and are comfortable with it: you pay the platform a fee and do the copy, photos and design yourself. We hand it to you finished, with copy included and a fixed price.
