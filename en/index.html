From the second year, €20 + VAT a month (€24.20 with VAT): renewal of a .com or .es domain, hosting and up to 30 minutes a month of small changes, non-cumulative. No tie-in. From the second year, the fee is charged monthly and you can cancel at hola@rotulito.com before the next payment. A further domain extension is quoted separately. These terms apply to the one-page plan; maintenance for custom projects is set out in its own quote.

Questions we get asked

The usual ones, answered straight.

Just one page? Don't I need more?

For most small businesses, no. People want to know what you do, where you are and how to reach you. If you ever need more, it can be expanded.

I already have Instagram and Google Maps.

Good: the website doesn't replace them, it's where they lead. Everything's organised there, with the call button, and it's yours even if an app changes its rules.

Is the domain mine?

Yes. It's registered in your name from day one. If you decide to leave, you take it with you.

Do I need to know about computers?

No. We handle the domain, the hosting and publishing it. You tell us about your business and send us the photos.

What exactly do I get for free?

A personalised visual proposal with your business details, so you can see the style and structure before paying anything. It's not the finished website: the final version comes later, with your final photos and copy.

What if I want to change something later?

The one-page plan includes up to 30 minutes a month, non-cumulative, to update hours, phone numbers, prices, short text or photos you send us, without changing the design. The first year is included; from the second year, it's part of the €20 + VAT monthly fee (€24.20 with VAT). Extensions, redesigns, new features and extra time are quoted separately, always with your approval first.

Do you build online shops?

Yes, but that's a different project and a different price. Tell us what you sell and we'll let you know.
