Why

Why your website matters

Social media and marketplaces are great for getting discovered, but they are not yours. Your website is.

01

It is the only thing you own

Instagram, Facebook or Marketplace change their rules, cut your reach or close your account, and you are left with nothing. Your website, your domain and your contacts are yours.

02

It is where people decide

Before calling you, people look you up to see who you are. A tidy, up-to-date website builds trust. One that is down or outdated raises doubts.

03

It works when you don’t

While you are working or asleep, it shows what you do, answers questions and collects quote requests.

Frequently asked questions

What about my Google profile and ad accounts?

They are yours too: in your name and with your details. We only have access to do the work. If we ever stop working together, everything stays yours.

I already have a website. Can you handle maintenance, SEO or ads?

Yes. We look at your website and your business and prepare a tailored quote with just what you really need.

How much does a website cost?

The one-page website costs €490 + VAT. A multi-page website is between €1,200 and €3,500 + VAT depending on pages and features, and custom development starts at €8,000 + VAT. Those two are quoted in writing according to scope before we start.

Does maintenance include SEO or ads?

No. The €35 + VAT/month maintenance is for the one-page website: domain, hosting and small changes. SEO, Google Business Profile and ads are quoted separately.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. Everything is done by WhatsApp and email: you tell us about your business, send the material and review the website on your phone. It doesn't matter where in Spain your business is.

Do you build websites in several languages?

Yes. Plata Marine, for example, is in four languages. We include it in the quote based on the number of pages.

Do you build online shops?

We look at what you sell and how many products you have, and tell you what suits you before quoting anything.

Is the domain mine?

Yes. It is registered in your name. If you decide to leave, you take it with you.

Do I have to pay before seeing anything?

Not for the one-page website. With what you tell us, we prepare a homepage proposal and show it to you. If you like it, you pay the first part and we carry on; if not, you owe us nothing. The proposal is shown as a preview, and the website becomes yours once it is paid for.

What does the one-page website not include?

Anything outside the package is quoted separately and before we start: an additional language (€150 + VAT), a simple logo (€250 + VAT) or professional email on your domain (€60 + VAT setup, plus the provider's fee).

Do you guarantee customers or being first on Google?

No. We prepare the website so search engines understand it and we can work on SEO and ads, but no website can guarantee rankings, visits or sales.
