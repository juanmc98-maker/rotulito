One price, that's it

490 € + VAT (592,90 € VAT included) for the published website, with domain and hosting for the first year. Half is paid when you say yes and the other half before publishing.

From the second year onwards, 20 € + VAT a month (24,20 € VAT included): renewing a .com or .es domain, hosting, and up to 30 minutes a month of small changes, non-cumulative. This same service is included during the first year. New pages or sections, redesigns, online stores, extra features and any work beyond the 30 minutes a month are quoted separately. We'll tell you the cost and wait for your go-ahead before starting any extra work.

No tie-in. From the second year, the fee is charged monthly and you can cancel by emailing hola@rotulito.com before the next charge. A different domain extension is quoted separately. These terms apply to the one-page plan; maintenance for custom projects is detailed in their own quote.

Questions people ask us

The usual ones, answered straight.

Just one page? Don't I need more?

For most small businesses, no. People want to know what you do, where you are and how to contact you. If you ever need more, it can be expanded.

I already have Instagram and Google Maps.

Good: the website doesn't replace them, it's where they lead. Everything's there in order, with the call button, and it's yours even if an app changes its rules.

Is the domain mine?

Yes. It's registered in your name from day one. If you decide to leave, you take it with you.

Do I need to know about computers?

No. We take care of the domain, the hosting and publishing it. You tell us about your business and send us the photos.

What if I want to change something later?

The one-page plan includes up to 30 minutes a month, non-cumulative, to update hours, phone numbers, prices, short texts or photos you send us, without changing the design. It's included in the first year; from the second year, it's part of the 20 € + VAT monthly fee (24,20 € VAT included). Expansions, redesigns, new features and any extra time are quoted separately, always with your prior approval.

Do you build online stores?

Yes, but that's a different project with a different price. Tell us what you sell and we'll let you know.
