Frequently asked questions

Just one page? Don't I need more?

For most small businesses, no. People want to know what you do, where you are and how to contact you. If you ever need more, we quote the extension.

I already have Instagram and Google Maps.

A website doesn't replace them: it completes them. It has all your information in one place, with a call button, and it's yours even if a social network changes its rules.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, what you offer and your photos (up to 12). If you don't have photos, we tell you which ones to take with your phone. We write the copy.

Is the domain mine?

Yes. It is registered in your name. If you decide to leave, you take it with you.

What happens after the first year?

From year two it is €20 + VAT a month, no minimum term: .com or .es domain, hosting and up to 30 minutes a month of small changes, which do not roll over. Anything else is quoted separately and before we start.

Do you guarantee customers or ranking first on Google?

No. We prepare the website so search engines understand it (titles, description and business details), but no website can guarantee rankings, visits or sales.

Do you work with businesses anywhere?

Yes, anywhere in Spain. Everything is done by WhatsApp and email.

Do you build online shops?

That's a different kind of project, with a different price. Tell us what you sell and we'll let you know.
