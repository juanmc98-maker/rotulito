Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need. As a guide: a landing page from €490 + VAT, a multi-section website between €1,200 and €3,500 + VAT and a large website from €8,000 + VAT. We give you the final price in writing before we start.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. Everything is done by WhatsApp and email: you tell us about your business, send the material and review the website on your phone. It doesn't matter where in Spain your business is.

Do you build websites in several languages?

Yes. Plata Marine, for example, is in four languages. We include it in the quote based on the number of pages.

Do you build online shops?

We look at what you sell and how many products you have, and tell you what suits you before quoting anything.

Is the domain mine?

Yes. It is registered in your name. If you decide to leave, you take it with you.

Do you guarantee customers or being first on Google?

No. We prepare the website so search engines understand it and we can work on SEO and ads, but no website can guarantee rankings, visits or sales.

Do you work with businesses anywhere?

Yes, all over Spain. Everything is done by WhatsApp and email.
