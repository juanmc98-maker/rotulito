FAQ

Do you guarantee results?

No. Nobody can guarantee Google rankings or a number of customers. What you do get is consistent work, full transparency and a monthly report on what is happening with your money.

Why a 3-month minimum?

Because campaigns need a few weeks to learn and local SEO takes time to show. With less time there is no data to decide on.

Is ad spend included?

No. You pay it directly to Google or Meta, with your card and in accounts in your name. Our fee is separate and fixed.

What happens if I stop?

You keep everything: accounts, campaigns, data and Business Profile, because they were always yours.

Do you work with any sector?

With almost all. If we see it will not pay off in your case, we tell you before starting.
