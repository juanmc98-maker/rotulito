What a carpentry website needs

Carpentry sells through the eyes: the website has to show what you do and make it easy to ask you for a quote.

Your work, with photos

A gallery of your real jobs (kitchens, wardrobes, doors, bespoke furniture…) so customers see your finish before they message you.

Quotes on WhatsApp

A button to message you directly. Customers can send measurements or a photo of the space, and you decide whether the job suits you.

What you do and where

Your services explained plainly and the area you cover, so you hear from people who can actually become your customers.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a carpentry website cost?

Build: €490 + VAT (€592.90 including VAT), in two payments of €245 + VAT. Includes design, copy, a .com or .es domain, hosting and maintenance for the first year. From year two, €35 + VAT per month (€42.35), with a 3-month minimum, renewable every 3 months.

How many photos of my work can I add?

Up to 12 photos on the one-page website. We help you choose the ones that best show your work and optimise them so they load fast. Swapping a photo later is covered by maintenance.

I already have Instagram with my work. Why do I need a website?

Instagram is great for showing your day-to-day, but not everyone uses it and it doesn't always make clear what you do, the area you cover or how to ask for a quote. The website sums that up on one page and can link to your Instagram.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. Everything is done over WhatsApp and email: you tell us about your business, send photos and review the site on your phone. It doesn't matter where in Spain you are.

Is the domain mine?

Yes. It's registered in your name. If you decide to leave, you take it with you.

Do you guarantee customers or first place on Google?

No. We prepare the site so search engines understand it (titles, description and business details), but no website can guarantee rankings, visits or sales.

A simple website for a carpentry business

A fitted kitchen or a piece of bespoke furniture is something people order only a few times in their lives, so they compare a lot before deciding. What makes them contact you is seeing work similar to theirs, well photographed, and understanding what kind of jobs you take on.

That's why a carpentry website is organised by what you make: kitchens, fitted wardrobes, doors, staircases or furniture for shops and restaurants, each with its own photos. If you work for homeowners and also for interior designers or builders, we keep them apart so each finds what they need.

We work remotely with workshops all over Spain: you send us photos of your work on WhatsApp and we write the copy.
