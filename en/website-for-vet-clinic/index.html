What pet owners ask before coming in

Many calls to a veterinary clinic are the same questions. The website can answer them at any time, including when the clinic is closed.

“Do you see emergencies?”

Whether you handle emergencies, at what times and which number to call. Clearly visible, right at the top.

“Do you see exotic pets?”

Which animals you treat (dogs and cats, rabbits, birds, reptiles…) and which you don't.

“How much is a consultation?”

If you like, guide prices for consultations and routine vaccinations; if not, people can ask by WhatsApp.

“Do I need an appointment?”

Whether you work by appointment, how to book and what to do if it can't wait.

“Do you have grooming or a shop?”

Any additional services, in a separate block.

“Do you do home visits?”

Whether you visit homes or farms, and in which area.

Which sections a veterinary clinic website needs (and why)

A clear page, designed for someone who may be worried about their animal.

  1. Home section with emergencies and phoneIf you handle emergencies, it is the first thing people should see, with a call button.
  2. Services and animals you treatConsultations, vaccinations, surgery, diagnostic imaging, grooming… and the species you see.
  3. The teamVets with their registration numbers and assistants, with real photos. It is reassuring to know who will treat the animal.
  4. The first visitWhat to bring (vaccination card or pet passport, previous tests) and what the first consultation is like.
  5. Hours, appointments and directionsNormal and emergency hours shown separately, WhatsApp to book and a map with the nearest parking.
  6. Legal detailsA legal notice with professional association and registration number, privacy and cookies.

To prepare it we only need your list of services, the species you treat, your opening hours (and emergency hours if any), photos of the clinic and the team, and your contact details.

Frequently asked questions

What legal information does it need?

A legal notice, privacy policy and cookie policy, like any business website. As veterinary medicine requires membership of a professional association in Spain, the law also requires stating the association, the membership number and the qualification. And prescription veterinary medicines can't be advertised to the public; we write the copy with that in mind.

Can you add an emergency notice that can be changed?

Yes. Changing the emergency hours or phone number is a small change covered by maintenance; just ask us on WhatsApp.

Does it include online appointments?

The one-page website doesn't include its own appointment system: clients book by phone or WhatsApp, or we link the button to the diary you already use. If you want customers to book on their own, see our website with online bookings.

Can we add vaccination or deworming reminders?

Yes, a short block in the home section that we change when you let us know, for example at the start of spring. It is a small change included in maintenance.

We have dog grooming and a shop. Do they fit on the same website?

Yes, each in its own block on the same page, with its own hours if they differ. If you want to sell products online, that is a separate project and we quote it separately.
