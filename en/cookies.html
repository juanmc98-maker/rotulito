Cookie policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Cookies and storage we use

We store your choice in rt_privacy_v2, in your browser's local storage, for 12 months. It records your decision on analytics and advertising, its date and the version of this notice. It is needed to respect your choice and is not used for advertising.

NameProvider and purposeDuration
_ga and _ga_*Google Analytics: browsing statistics, only with your analytics consent.Up to 12 months from creation.
_gcl_*Google Ads: measures how effective our ads are (which ad brought the visit), only with your advertising consent.Up to 90 days from creation.
_fbp and _fbcMeta/Facebook (pixel): measures how effective our ads on Facebook and Instagram are, only with your advertising consent.Up to 90 days from creation.

If you accept analytics only, we do not enable any advertising cookies. If you also accept advertising, we let Google Ads and Meta/Facebook measure which visits and contacts (for example, a WhatsApp click or a submitted form) come from our ads; we do not use this data to build an advertising profile beyond that measurement or share your identity with third parties. In analytics we only send the page address, campaign parameters (utm) and the domain of the referring website, never form data. Rejecting analytics or advertising does not stop you from using the forms or contacting us.

After a confirmed submission, a timestamp in session storage lets us show the thank-you page. It does not contain form data and is deleted once shown.

Change or withdraw your consent

The controls are also in the footer. If you withdraw consent, that measurement is switched off and the related cookies accessible to this website are deleted; if the tool was already loaded, the page reloads to stop it. You can also clear the site data from your browser.

Data and transfers

Google Analytics, Google Ads and the Meta/Facebook pixel process browsing information and pseudonymous identifiers, which should not be confused with anonymous information. See Google's transfer safeguards, Meta's privacy policy and our privacy policy, which identifies the data controller and explains your rights.
