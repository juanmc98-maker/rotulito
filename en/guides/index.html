How ours works, in 3 steps

The whole process happens over WhatsApp and email, with no meetings.

1

You tell us about your business

Fill in the form or message us on WhatsApp. We confirm whether a one-page website is what you need and put the price, maintenance and what's included in writing. If it suits you, you pay 50 % and we start.

2

We design and write your site

You send photos from your phone (up to 12), your logo if you have one and your contact details. We write the copy and send you the finished site to review. Up to two rounds of changes.

3

We publish it and submit it to Google

Once you approve it, you pay the remaining 50 % and we go live: we register your domain, publish the site, link it to your Google listing if you have one and submit it to Google for indexing. Then we explain how to ask us for changes.
