How it works, in 3 steps

The whole process is done by WhatsApp and email, with no meetings. You tell us about your business; we do the rest.

1

You tell us about your business

Fill in the form or message us on WhatsApp. We confirm whether a one-page website is what you need and send you the price, maintenance and what is included in writing. If it suits you, you pay 50% and we get started.

2

We design and write your website

You send us photos of finished jobs, ideally with before and after shots (up to 12), your logo if you have one, your services, your area and your hours. We write the copy and send you the finished website to review. Up to two rounds of changes.

3

We publish it and submit it to Google

Once you approve it, you pay the remaining 50% and we go live: we register your domain, publish the website, link it to your Google Business Profile if you have one and submit it to Google for indexing. Then we explain how to request changes.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website for a builder or renovation business cost?

Build: €490 + VAT (€592.90 including VAT), paid in two instalments of €245 + VAT. It includes design, copywriting, a .com or .es domain, hosting and maintenance for the first year. From the second year, €20 + VAT a month (€24.20), with no minimum term.

I'm already on renovation directories, why do I need a website?

Directories can bring you enquiries, but there you appear next to other professionals. Your website is yours alone: you can put the link on your quotes, on your van or on your Google profile so that anyone looking you up sees your work and contacts you directly.

Can I show prices?

Every renovation job is different, so most builders don't show fixed prices and quote after seeing the work. If you like, you can show a guide price. It is up to you.

Can I say I'm insured or registered with the REA?

Yes, if you can prove it. Public liability insurance or registration with Spain's Register of Accredited Companies (REA), which in some cases is required to work as a contractor or subcontractor on construction sites, gives customers confidence. We only include what you really have; if you are unsure whether it applies to you, check with your accountant.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. Everything is done by WhatsApp and email: you tell us about your business, send us photos and review the website on your phone. It doesn't matter where in Spain you are.

Is the domain mine?

Yes. It is registered in your name. If you ever leave, you take it with you.

Do you guarantee customers or the top spot on Google?

No. We prepare the website so search engines understand it (titles, description and business details), but no website can guarantee rankings, visits or sales.
